# Course terminology

- LCM means a 50-metre pool.
- SCM means a 25-metre pool.
- SCY means a 25-yard pool.
- World Aquatics world records apply to recognized metric courses. Never call an SCY benchmark a world record.
- A modeled course conversion is a planning estimate, not automatically a legal meet-entry time. The swimmer or coach must check the meet’s governing rules.

# Evidence hierarchy

1. Entered measured splits and official source data.
2. Deterministic SetCraft calculations.
3. Closely matched official reference races selected by SetCraft.
4. Coach-entered observations and measurements, labeled with their protocol.
5. Self-ratings and subjective comments.
6. Modeled or estimated checkpoints, clearly labeled.

Do not present a lower evidence layer as if it were a higher one. Separate observation from interpretation and uncertainty.

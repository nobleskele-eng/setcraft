# LCM

LCM uses 50 m lengths. Strategy places more uninterrupted emphasis on free-swimming rhythm than short course. Starts, turns, and transitions remain important, but there are fewer turns over the same metric race distance.

Coach checks:

- can the athlete preserve rhythm through the longer free-swimming segment?
- do breathing and tempo plans survive without a wall reset?
- is the single mid-race turn or limited set of turns executed at race speed?
- are LCM checkpoints measured or converted?

# SCM

SCM uses 25 m lengths. More turns change the distribution of underwater and surface work. Metric event distances match LCM labels, but performance is not directly interchangeable.

Coach checks:

- repeated turn consistency;
- breakout speed relative to surface speed;
- legal underwater distance;
- ability to reconnect the stroke after every wall;
- whether the strategy relies on modeled LCM conversion.

# SCY

SCY uses 25 yd lengths. Use SCY-specific checkpoints and benchmarks when available. Never call an SCY performance a World Aquatics world record. Labels may include meet record, program record, NCAA benchmark, national benchmark, or U.S. Open benchmark when verified by deterministic data.

Coach checks:

- turn density and repeated wall skill;
- yard-specific segment timing;
- event differences such as 500/1000/1650 freestyle;
- whether a metric comparison is planning-only.

# Conversion boundary

A converted time is for **planning** unless the governing meet explicitly accepts that conversion and method. The coach or swimmer must check the current meet package, federation rules, qualifying period, proof requirements, event, category, and course.

Never say a planning conversion is guaranteed for entry. Never overwrite an official or measured time with a converted value.

# Benchmark language

- World Aquatics world records apply to recognized metric-course records under current rules.
- SCY references must carry their actual organization and label.
- A reference race can guide shape without becoming the athlete's target.
- Explain course mismatch explicitly.

# Strategy transfer across courses

Transfer the underlying questions, not raw splits:

- opening control;
- turn and underwater contribution;
- free-swimming rhythm;
- mid-race stability;
- closing execution.

Then use SetCraft's locked course-specific checkpoints. Do not manually scale every segment in prose.

# Rule currency

Current World Aquatics and governing meet rules control legal execution and records. Knowledge files may become outdated, so a rules-sensitive answer must check the current deterministic rule source.

# Race analysis

Use the splits, checkpoint provenance and comparisons supplied by SetCraft. Do not recalculate or replace them.

Organize the explanation around:

- overall result and evidence quality;
- where pace changed relative to the swimmer’s own race;
- how turns, underwaters or stroke phases may have contributed when actual observations support that statement;
- what the closest reference races demonstrate without treating correlation as causation;
- two or three prioritized coach checks for the next video review or validation set.

Measured checkpoints and modeled checkpoints must stay visibly separate. A race pattern may be consistent with several causes; do not diagnose conditioning, lactate response, technique or motivation from splits alone.
